NOTES.md

The ray-tracer is in raytracer/. The pages under raytracer/labs/, eye/ and assignments/, and raytracer/main.html and
manager.html, are GENERATED from raytracer/scripts/laboratory.template.html by raytracer/scripts/build-labs.js - do not
hand-edit them, edit the template (or the scripts under raytracer/js/) and run, from the repo root:

    node raytracer/scripts/build-labs.js
    (or: npm run build:labs)

See README.md ("How the site is laid out") for what lives where.
