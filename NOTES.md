NOTES.md

The pages under labs/, eye/ and assignments/, and main.html and manager.html, are GENERATED from
scripts/laboratory.template.html by scripts/build-labs.js - do not hand-edit them, edit the template (or the
linked js/optics/*.js files) and run, from the repo root:

    node scripts/build-labs.js
    (or: npm run build:labs)

See README.md ("How the site is laid out") for what lives where.
