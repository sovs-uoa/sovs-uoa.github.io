A localized patch is not a single spectral point but a small Gaussian blob centred on its carrier frequency (at θ and θ+180°, since a real-valued grating always has a mirrored twin) — this is the same time–frequency uncertainty tradeoff behind the gap between the low/high bands in the 4×4 Gabor grid lab. Narrowing σ shrinks the patch but spreads the blob; raising the frequency slides the blob pair further from DC. Drag the patch (or use the position sliders) to move it around the canvas — by the Fourier shift theorem this only rotates the phase spectrum; the amplitude spectrum stays exactly the same.
