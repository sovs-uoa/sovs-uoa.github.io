3b. Notch filter (periodic-noise removal)

Rejects a small square patch of frequencies at a chosen frequency and orientation — the classic "manual editing" fix for periodic noise (Lecture 7, Fig 7.10). Since a real image's spectrum is symmetric about DC, a mirrored square is added automatically on the opposite side. "Extend to edge" turns each square into a radial spoke running outward to the edge of the spectrum (away from DC, not toward it) — useful when noise energy smears along a whole radial direction rather than staying isolated at one frequency. Combines with whatever filter is set above (or use it alone with Filter = None).
